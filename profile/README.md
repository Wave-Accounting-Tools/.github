## 💼 What Wave Accounting Brings to Small Business Workflows

Wave Accounting is a financial management platform designed to help small businesses organise bookkeeping, invoicing, and everyday accounting activities. Its tools bring several common administrative tasks into a shared workspace.

The invoicing features help businesses prepare customer bills and maintain records of amounts due. Organised invoice information can make it easier to review billing history and follow up on outstanding payments.

Bookkeeping functions support the recording and categorisation of business income and expenses. Consistent transaction records can help business owners understand routine financial activity and prepare information for accounting reviews.

Expense tracking helps users organise business costs and maintain a clearer view of spending. Keeping expense records together can simplify administrative reviews and provide supporting information for bookkeeping.

Receipt management can help preserve documentation associated with business purchases. Depending on the available feature and subscription, receipt capture and related tools can support the process of connecting purchase records with expenses.

Customer records provide a place to maintain relevant billing information. Keeping customer details organised can reduce repetitive administrative work when preparing invoices and reviewing previous transactions.

Payment tracking helps users follow the status of invoices and recorded transactions. The precise payment methods and processing options available depend on the user's location, account configuration, and current Wave service offerings.

Financial reports provide summaries of recorded business activity. These reports can help owners review income, expenses, and other accounting information when monitoring operations or preparing records for professional review.

Wave's features may also support collaboration with accountants or bookkeepers, depending on account permissions and the available service options. Sharing organised records can make it easier to coordinate bookkeeping tasks and review financial information.

Small businesses can use accounting records to prepare for tax-related administrative work. However, tax requirements vary by jurisdiction, and users should verify reporting obligations with a qualified professional when necessary.

Wave has offered payroll features in supported markets, but availability, fees, tax services, and functionality can vary by location and product offering. Businesses should confirm current eligibility and service details before planning payroll workflows around the platform.

Users researching Wave may also compare it with other accounting products or investigate moving records between systems. Before transferring data, it is important to check supported import formats, field compatibility, and whether invoices, transactions, and customer records will retain the information needed.

Wave's suitability depends on business size, location, accounting needs, and the specific features available under the current service. Reviewing pricing, payment processing charges, payroll availability, and account limitations can help determine whether the platform fits an organisation's workflow.
